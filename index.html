03 / My approach

Think in risks.
Test for outcomes.

Quality is not a final checkpoint. It is an engineering practice that connects product expectations to evidence.

01 / DISCOVER

Understand the risk

Identify business-critical paths, data dependencies and costly failure modes.

02 / DESIGN

Choose the right coverage

Combine focused exploration, boundaries, API contracts and automation.

03 / EXECUTE

Produce reliable evidence

Capture reproducible failures, traces and results that engineers can act on.

04 / IMPROVE

Close the feedback loop

Prioritize defects, track recurring risks and strengthen regression coverage.
